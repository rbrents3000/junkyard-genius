# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 2026

Junkyard Genius is a free, open-source DIY project guide hosted at [junkyard.build](https://junkyard.build). This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## Analytics

We use **Google Analytics 4** (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. GA4 collects:

- Pages visited and time spent
- Device type, browser, and general location (country/region)
- Interactions such as search queries, filter usage, and button clicks

GA4 uses cookies (`_ga`, `_gid`) to distinguish unique visitors. **No personally identifiable information** (name, email, address) is collected through analytics.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Local Storage

The site uses your browser's **localStorage** to remember:

- Builds you've marked as "Completed"
- Builds on your "Want to Build" list

This data stays entirely in your browser. It is never sent to any server.

## Cookies

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics visitor ID | 2 years |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics session ID | 24 hours |

No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Analytics** — analytics (see above)
- **Google Fonts** — the Inter typeface is loaded from Google's CDN
- **Vercel** — hosting provider

## Data We Do Not Collect

- Email addresses (no signup or newsletter)
- Payment information
- Personal information of any kind
