> Builds that break brains through your eyeballs. Every project here exists for one reason: to make people physically stop and stare.

This category is for builds where the visual impact is the entire point. These aren't tools or instruments — they're spectacles. Conversation pieces. The things that make someone walk into your workshop and say "wait, you made that from *what*?" Every build here scores ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ on both Jaw Drop and Clout Potential, because if it doesn't make people's eyes go wide, it doesn't belong here.

The builds cross multiple disciplines — physics, chemistry, electronics, mechanical engineering — but they're unified by one trait: they produce something visually extraordinary from salvaged junk.


### Suggested Build Order

Start with the most accessible, build toward the most complex:

1. **[#337 — Infinity Room](337-infinity-room/)** — Mirrors + LEDs. No electronics beyond plugging in a strip. Instant wow.
2. **[#334 — Water Vortex Table](334-water-vortex-table/)** — Pump + acrylic. Basic plumbing, spectacular result.
3. **[#336 — Water Droplet Time Fountain](336-water-droplet-time-fountain/)** — Arduino intro. Strobe timing is the magic trick.
4. **[#330 — Cloud Chamber Coffee Table](330-cloud-chamber-coffee-table/)** — Physics meets furniture. Dry ice handling required.
5. **[#332 — Dead Appliance Rube Goldberg](332-dead-appliance-rube-goldberg/)** — Pure mechanical creativity. Time is the limiting factor.
6. **[#333 — Ferrofluid Wall](333-ferrofluid-wall/)** — Electromagnet grid + Arduino. Intermediate electronics.
7. **[#335 — CRT Electron Art Array](335-crt-electron-art-array/)** — High voltage CRT work. Respect the flyback.
8. **[#331 — Fire Organ](331-fire-organ/)** — Propane + electronics + music. Maximum spectacle, maximum respect required.
9. **[#338 — Mechanical Flip-Dot Display](338-mechanical-flip-dot-display/)** — 256 hand-wound coils. The Everest of this category.
