# The Reverse Cookbook

> You don't need a project to start. You just need a part. Look up what you HAVE and find out what you can BUILD.

Traditional cookbooks go: "I want to make X, here are the ingredients." This is the opposite. You've got a box of stepper motors, a bag of neodymium magnets, and half a bottle of copper sulfate. What can you make? This index tells you.

Every entry lists the builds that use that ingredient, plus where to get more of it. Sorted alphabetically because your junk drawer isn't organized either, but at least this page can be.
